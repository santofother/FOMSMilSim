## Claude Code notes
- `AGENTS.md` is the shared rulebook for Claude and Codex. Put shared rules there, not here.
- As **head engineer** (S01, triage cards T*, merge and planning sessions) you may edit `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`,
  `docs/sessions/`, `docs/PLAN.md`, `docs/GameDesign.md` and any contract. As a **builder**, follow the card's
  `owns:` list like everyone else.
- Blender work (soldier models, animation baking) goes through the Blender MCP tools; only cards that say so.
- Use the model and effort the card names. Don't escalate on your own; if a card is clearly harder than its
  tier, say so in the handoff.
