# S05 — Morale, routing, rallying, the general

## What exists now
- `Morale/MoraleSystem.cs` + `MoraleRules.cs` replace the M0 45% rule. Each tick a **target** = unit morale + modifiers (losses so far, recent loss *rate*, flank/rear share of blows taken, winning/losing the melee, friends routing within 40 m, shock when a friend breaks nearby, the army is beaten (share of the team's men out), general near (60 m) / in sight (150 m) / killed, rallied boost, fresh/tired/exhausted). Morale falls toward it at 8/s and rises at 1.5/s. States Eager ≥ 80, Steady ≥ 40, Wavering ≥ 15 (5-point hysteresis), else Broken.
- Broken regiments drop their order, ignore new ones, and run to their side's edge (behind the army as deployed, i.e. −mean spawn facing). When all their men are Gone, `Regiment.Fled` = true. An explicit `Attack` may pursue routers; auto-attack doesn't.
- Rally: broken, no enemy within 80 m for 6 s, general within 60 m (or a rally order in the last 15 s) → re-forms facing the nearest enemy, morale 25, `Activity = Rallying` for 4 s (orders refused). The second break = Shattered, never rallies.
- General: `gb_general` (trait `"general"`). The commander is one soldier (second rank, middle). `UseAbility(gen, "rally", target)` gives +15 now plus +30 fading over 30 s, range 70 m, cooldown 45 s, only for a Wavering or Broken target. When he dies: `GeneralKilled`, army −20 shock fading to a lasting −8, and no general bonus.
- m0_clash now ends in 65–78 s (16 seeds). Front regiments break at roughly 45–55% strength, reserves follow.

## Public API (what other cards can use)
- `Battle.Morale` (`MoraleSystem`): `Commander`, `GeneralOf`, `GeneralDead`, `Cooldown`, `Breaks`, `FleeDir`, `Centroid`, `Accepts`. `Regiment.MoraleReasons` (list of `MoraleReason(Label, Value)`, first = unit morale), `Regiment.Fled`.
- Events `RegimentWavering`, `RegimentRallied`, `RegimentShattered`, `GeneralKilled` (+ existing `RegimentRouted`). `MoraleState.Broken` (`Routing` stays as an alias). S06 charge shock: add a modifier in `MoraleSystem.Target`, or call in with a shock like `_shock`.
