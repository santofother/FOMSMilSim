# S04b — Formation movement

## What exists now
- `Movement/FormationSteering.cs`: Move and Attack approach share one steering step. Targets ≤ 60° off the facing are reached by wheeling on the march (rate 1.5 × pace / frontage, about the front, moving along the facing, so the inner flank never walks backwards). Bigger turns face about the formation's middle at once. Inside 6 m (or the formation's depth), the regiment walks straight to the dragged spot; final facing differences ≤ 30° turn gradually about the middle, bigger ones snap.
- Lateral move with the facing kept = flank march: the block slides sideways, men turn to walk, the facing never changes.
- `Movement/FormationSlots.cs`: when the facing jumps (> 0.35 rad in a tick) or the frontage changes, men take home slots by position (front-most men form the front rank, left to right). A 180° turn is an about-face: the rear rank becomes the front. S04's column fill runs on top of these homes.
- Men more than 1.5 m from their slot hurry (up to 1.8 × pace, capped at run speed): the outer flank of a wheel, re-forming.
- An arrived Move order holds: pushes and follow-ups now work on it (S04 known issue), and it no longer walks back.
- Per-soldier yaw already followed velocity while walking (S01/S04). Unchanged.

## Public API (what other cards can use)
- None new. `Move`/`Attack`/`Withdraw` unchanged. `Withdraw` and turning while in contact keep S02's turn about the front.

## Tests

## Manual check (for the user, max 3 lines)
- Right-click 45° to a line's left: it curves there without stopping. Click 90° to the side: men face that way and march.
- Drag a facing 180° from the current one on the same spot: the line faces about, nobody spins.
- Drag a far destination keeping the facing: the block marches sideways and arrives already facing right.
