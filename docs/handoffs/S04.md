# S04 — Melee v2 + fatigue

## What exists now
- `Combat/Melee.cs`: per-soldier duels. Hit = 0.4 + attack − defence (× fatigue) + flank 0.2 / rear 0.3, clamped 5–95%. Side comes from the **defender regiment's** facing (±60° front). A shield blocks `shield` of frontal hits only. Damage = weapon_damage × (1 − ½–1 × armour%) + armour_piercing, ±20%.
- `Movement/MeleeSpacing.cs`: column-fill slots (a man falls, the men behind him step up). Soldiers step toward enemies only on a 0.8 m leash (attacker front rank: 2.5 m, to lap round the ends). Enemies keep a 0.95 m contact gap. A heavier regiment drives a lighter one back (0.6 m/s × (mA−mB)/(mA+mB)). An attacker whose front men can't reach steps into the gap, but never past the nearest enemy.
- `Movement/FatigueRules.cs`: run +0.010/s, fight +0.006/s, walk +0.0005/s, rest −0.008/s (× 100/stamina). Bands fresh/winded/tired/exhausted at 0.25/0.5/0.75 → attack & defence ×1/.92/.8/.62, speed ×1/.95/.85/.7.
- The M0 fields `melee_hit_chance` and `weapon_damage_random` are no longer read. They stay in the JSON because the loader requires them. Triage can drop them.

## Public API (what other cards can use)
- `EventKind.MeleeHit`: Soldier = attacker, Regiment = attacker's regiment, Other = victim, Pos = victim. `MeleeRules.Killed(battle, e)` tells you whether the blow killed (valid until the next Step).
- `MeleeRules` (SideOf, HitChance, ShieldBlock, Damage, constants), `MeleeSide`, `FatigueRules` (BandOf, CombatMul, SpeedMul, rates), `FatigueBand`. `Regiment.Fatigue` is now live (the cards bar shows it).
