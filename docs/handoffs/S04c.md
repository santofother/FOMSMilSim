# S04c — Regiment contact

## What exists now
- `Movement/RegimentContact.cs`: formation boxes (through the outer men, ranks counted from the living), box gap (SAT), face choice (front/flanks/rear, score = distance off the face ÷ (half extent + 10 m), switch only when 1.25× better).
- Attack / auto-attack (`RegimentOrders.CloseWith` + `Plan`): pick a face, then a stretch of it no friend has claimed (slide 1.5 m steps, ≥ 6 m shared front), re-planned every 10 ticks until in contact, then locked. Steer at a carrot 8 m out on the face's normal → lines meet square. A longer attacker centres on the face. Two regiments attacking each other from the front meet front to front. No snap re-face within 15 m of the enemy.
- Keep-clear after every order step (not Withdraw): enemies at 0.8 × own reach, friends at 1 m. A friend in the way is side-stepped (±45°/90°); an attacker also slides round an enemy corner; a `Move` that runs into an enemy ends there (Fighting, TargetRegiment set). Steps that don't worsen an existing overlap, and turns toward your own target, are always allowed.
- New `Move`s whose destination overlaps a friend's (destination, or where it stands) slide sideways along their frontage until clear (after all of the tick's orders, so group orders see each other).
- Men fight whoever is in reach whatever the regiment is doing (`Battle.UpdateSoldier`), except when withdrawing.

## Public API (what other cards can use)

## Manual check (for the user, max 3 lines)
- Attack an enemy line from 45° off: it swings round and hits front to front, not corner first.
- Order a line straight through an enemy: it stops at their front and fights. Order two of yours across each other: they step round.
- Let Red come at you in `sandbox_vs_ai`: lines stay lines; a second Red regiment goes for a free flank, not through its friend.
